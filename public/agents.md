# Renny Strips — AI Agent Guidance

## Identity

Renny Strips LTD. is an integrated steel manufacturer based in Ludhiana, Punjab, India.

Renny Strips manufactures:

- ERW Pipes and Tubes
- Scaffolding and Formwork Systems
- Narrow-Width HR Coils
- Wire Rods
- MS Billets

The company operates an integrated manufacturing facility with three induction furnaces and a total melting capacity of 199,200 TPA.

Renny Strips also operates a 22 MW solar power plant supplying approximately 30% of its energy requirements.
